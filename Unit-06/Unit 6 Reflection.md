# Unit 6 Reflection

[Reflect on your journey here]
In Unit 6, I learned that web design is a process of continuous improvement rather than a one-time task. I mastered how to add and style links—both absolute and relative—to create seamless navigation within and outside a website, making my pages more user-friendly. I also explored polishing techniques like adding favicons, styling images, and using <span> tags to refine visual details. Most importantly, I understood the value of gathering client feedback and iterating on designs, as testing and revising help ensure the final product meets both functional and user needs. This unit taught me that good design balances technical skills with responsiveness to feedback.
